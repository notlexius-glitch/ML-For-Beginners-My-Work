
# History of Machine Learning

## Important Dates

### 1950 - Machines that Think

- Alan Turing introduced the idea of thinking machines.
- Created the Turing Test.

### 1956 - Dartmouth Conference

- The term Artificial Intelligence was introduced.
- Important people: John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky.

### 1956-1974 - Golden Years

- AI research grew quickly.
- Robots and chatbots were developed.

### 1974-1980 - AI Winter

- AI progress slowed because:
  - Low computer power
  - Lack of data
  - High expectations

### 1980s - Expert Systems

- AI systems based on rules were used by companies.

### 1993-2011

- More data and powerful computers improved ML.

### Today

- ML is used in healthcare, finance, recommendation systems and many other areas.

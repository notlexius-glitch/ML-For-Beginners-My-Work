
# Lesson 3 Challenge

## Preventing Harmful AI Systems

To prevent irresponsible AI:

1. Use diverse and representative datasets.
2. Test AI models for bias and unfair decisions.
3. Include different perspectives during development.
4. Protect user privacy and security.
5. Continuously monitor AI after deployment.

## Real Life Example

A hiring AI system may reject qualified candidates if the training data contains historical bias.

Solution:

- Check training data
- Test fairness
- Monitor model performance
- Keep humans involved in important decisions

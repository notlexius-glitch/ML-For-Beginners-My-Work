
# Lesson 3 Notes: Building Machine Learning Solutions with Responsible AI

## Responsible AI

Responsible AI means creating AI systems that are fair, reliable, safe, inclusive, transparent, secure, and accountable.

## Fairness

AI systems should treat everyone equally and avoid biased decisions.

Examples of unfair AI:

- Gender bias
- Racial bias
- Stereotyping
- Unequal quality of service

## Reliability and Safety

AI systems should work correctly in normal and unexpected situations.

Example:
A self-driving car AI should handle:

- Weather changes
- Pedestrians
- Road conditions
- Unexpected obstacles

## Inclusiveness

AI systems should be designed for everyone, including people with disabilities.

## Privacy and Security

AI systems must:

- Protect personal information
- Secure training data
- Prevent unauthorized access

## Transparency

AI models should be understandable.

Developers should explain:

- How the model works
- Why it made a decision
- What limitations it has

## Accountability

Developers and organizations are responsible for AI system outcomes.

## Impact Assessment

Before deploying AI systems, we should check:

- Possible harms
- Data quality
- User impact
- Safety risks

## Responsible AI Debugging

Responsible AI tools help analyze:

- Model errors
- Bias
- Data problems
- Model decisions
